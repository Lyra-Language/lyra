Full-width wrap/saturate is one native op / `llvm.*.sat` intrinsic (same lowering the methods need), but arbitrary-range saturation is compare+select after each op and ties into the range/interval-analysis TODO above. **Sequencing:** hold the general form until the trap-on-overflow default exists (the trap/wrap/saturate trichotomy only coheres once "trap" is real); **wrapping-only** is the cheap, unambiguous first slice (native add, no range machinery).

Parallel `ref` borrows get their no-mutation guarantee from `pure`/`det`, not a new checker.
